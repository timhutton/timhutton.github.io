title: Finally imported my Twitter archive into my Jekyll blog!
---

So it took me four years of procrastination but I finally used [my own tool](https://github.com/timhutton/twitter-archive-parser) to convert my tweets into markdown files and import them into my Jekyll-hosted blog which (at the time of writing!) is what you're reading now.

You'll see them collected into a single post each month, since tweets are typically short.

The only bit I need to fix is that Jekyll doesn't support the gtihub-flavor of markdown where bare URLs are automatically made into hyperlinks. But that's not the end of the world.

Really the only reason for doing this at all is to help me to find those things that I remember mentioning or talking about, and for the long tail of search.
